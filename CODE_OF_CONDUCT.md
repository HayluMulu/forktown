# Be a good neighbor

Forktown welcomes contributors of every experience level and background. Be patient with beginners, offer specific and constructive feedback, respect others’ work and identities, and give credit honestly.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, sexual content directed at others, publishing someone’s private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not welcome. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and contributed place content.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive contributions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate and explained when appropriate. Respect privacy when raising or handling concerns.

For ordinary project concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository’s available channels. A private reporting contact must be listed here before the public community launch; do not post sensitive reports or personal details in a public issue. GitHub’s built-in reporting tools can be used for platform abuse.

Everyone starts somewhere. Help the next person feel welcome.
